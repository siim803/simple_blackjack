BLACKJACK 21

THE GAME

Objective: Get a hand value closer to 21 than the dealer without going over.

  • Numbered Cards: Worth their face value
  • J, Q, K: Worth 10 points
  • Ace: Worth 1 or 11 points

If your hand exceeds 21, you bust and lose immediately. The dealer then reveals their hidden card and draws according to the rules.

RULES

  • Blackjack: 21 with first 2 cards (Ace + 10-value card)
  • Bust: Hand exceeds 21 and you lose
  • Dealer Must Hit: Dealer hits on 16 or lower
  • Dealer Must Stand: Dealer stands on 17 or higher
  • Push/Tie: Same hand value as dealer
  • Soft Hand: Hand with Ace counted as 11
  • Hard Hand: Hand without Ace or Ace as 1
  • Win Condition: Closer to 21 than dealer without bust

CONTROLS

  • HIT Draw another card to your hand
  • STAND End your turn and let dealer play
  • SPLIT Split your hand (limited support)
  • RESTART Start a new round

Good luck, and remember to play responsibly!
